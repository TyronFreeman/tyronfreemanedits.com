Plain words about your details.

Tyron Freeman Edits is run by me, Tyron Freeman, a sole trader in Wānaka, New Zealand. This page says what I collect, why, and how to reach me.

Contact

What I collect

  • The application form: your name, email, channel, and your answers about your channel and editing.
  • A booked call: your name, email and the time you picked.
  • Emails you send me.

Why

To see if we are a fit, to reply to you, and to run the work if we go ahead. I do not sell your details or pass them to anyone for marketing.

Where it is kept

The form runs on Tally, bookings on Cal.com, and email on Google. They hold the details on my behalf. If we do not end up working together, I delete your application within 12 months.

This website

This site sets no cookies of its own and has no trackers. The fonts come from Google Fonts, the form from Tally, and the video from YouTube when you press play. Those services see your visit the way any website does.

If I emailed you first

I only write to business addresses that creators have published themselves. Reply “stop” and I will not write again.

Your rights

You can ask to see what I hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Email me and I will do it. This follows the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.
